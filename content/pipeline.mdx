<span className="sn">3.2</span>
<h3>The transplant probe <span className="tg">&mdash; does the trigger travel?</span></h3>
<div>A poison patch is cut from one image and pasted onto a fresh background where it has never appeared. <span className="sn">3.6</span>
<h3>Confidence remap <span className="tg">&mdash; the guidance program</span></h3>
<div>The three signals fuse into <span className="im r">p_poison</span> (Eq. 2). High p_poison on a strong box is <em>demoted to &epsilon;</em> rather than deleted; low p_poison is trusted as-is; the region between ramps down; noise-floor boxes are dropped outright (Eq. 3). This surface is built as an interactive apparatus in Chapter IV.</div>
